## 1. Trade-off memo

> **Rock 1:** Run the Platform (supports KR1)
>
> **Rock 2:** Introduce Concept X (supports KR3)
>
> **Rock 3:** Introduce MVP Virtual Brand (Interim) (supports KR3)
>
> **Hard no 1:** Multiple optimization  for current platform: No substantial investments in old tech which will be renewed/replatformed
>
> **Hard no 2:** Optimize Marketing & Loyalty Solution: Marketing & Loyalty Solution is mature, stable and ready to use. Only if data /business case overrules other initiatives
>
> **Hard no 3:** Top-up Charity Donations: Great brand idea, but there are (easier/cheaper) Alternatives to reach the outcome

## 2. Outcome roadmap

| Horizon | Outcome / bet | Owning team(s) | Success signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Now (0 to 3 mo) | 1. Reliable platform (KR1) — We bet that strengthening operational reliability and control will protect revenue and ensure uninterrupted customer and store journeys for customers, franchisees and employees. | Platform, Product, Key Vendors, Operations | ≥99.9% availability across critical journeys; agreed incident recovery targets met; fewer critical incidents.
| Next (3 to 6 mo) | 1. Modernise the platform foundation — We bet that reducing critical legacy dependencies and architectural complexity will make the platform more reliable and adaptable. | Platform, Architecture, Key Vendors | Reduction in critical legacy dependencies; improved maintainability; no deterioration in availability.
| Later (6 to 12 mo) | 1. Enable multi-country expansion — a bet: We bet that a configurable and reusable platform foundation will reduce the time, cost and complexity of entering new countries. | Product, Platform, Architecture, Group | Reusable country capabilities; lower localisation effort; faster market entry.
