# Team Charter: More than Dough:  a platform and business designed for the next stage of growth.

> Module 3 · Set the Habits That Make a Team Faster

## 1. What We Own

> **We own:** The Product team owns the end-to-end enabling platform journey, from first customer click to repeat order, across Marketing & Loyalty, Commerce, Store Operations and Data & AI. Each PM owns the outcomes, roadmap and priorities of their domain, working in cross-functional teams with business stakeholders, UX, Architecture and suppliers; the Head of Platform owns the overall platform strategy, portfolio priorities and cross-domain trade-offs. The Content & Config team is the primary platform user, and our product decisions must enable them to manage brands, campaigns and platform configuration effectively.
>
> **Out of scope:** The Data team owns enterprise data infrastructure, data management and data governance; the Governance function owns enterprise-wide policies, standards and compliance. Our team owns the product requirements and platform integration with these capabilities, but not their delivery priorities or functional ownership. Suppliers own technical implementation and delivery commitments, not product direction or business priorities.
>
> **Joint calls:** Decisions affecting multiple product domains, shared platform capabilities, store operating models or the experience of multiple brands require input from the relevant PMs and business owners before commitment. Decisions affecting international expansion, group technology standards, material architecture choices or investment beyond the agreed budget require a joint decision with Group, Architecture, Finance or the Board, as relevant. No individual PM or supplier can unilaterally commit the platform to a cross-domain or group-wide change.

## 2. How We Decide

> **Who decides scope:** The relevant PM decides features, backlog priorities and scope within their domain and agreed strategic objectives, budget and architectural guardrails. The Head of Platform makes the final call on cross-domain priorities, shared platform capabilities and trade-offs between Run, Grow and Transform; the Board approves strategic direction, major investments and changes to agreed funding. Suppliers advise on feasibility, cost and delivery options but do not decide what gets built or why.
>
> **How cross-team conflicts escalate:** PMs first attempt to resolve conflicts directly with the affected PMs and stakeholders, bringing unresolved priority, capacity or scope conflicts to the Head of Platform within two working days. The Head of Platform makes the final decision on product and portfolio trade-offs within five working days; conflicts involving strategic direction, funding or group-wide standards are escalated to the Board, which makes the final call. Until a decision is made, no team or supplier unilaterally changes agreed scope, priorities or commitments.
