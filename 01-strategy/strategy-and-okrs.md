## 2. Your one hard no

> **Hard no:** 1. No reliability compromises:  Never sacrifice platform stability for short-term gains or faster delivery. 2. No big-bang transformations or endless legacy patching: Modernise progressively, without replacing everything at once or endlessly fixing the past. 3. No commerce-only optimisation: Never prioritise online sales at the expense of the wider business, including franchisees and store operations. 4. No unnecessary complexity: Avoid overly complex architectures, redundant systems and unnecessary customisation. 5. No loss of strategic control: Avoid isolated solutions and vendor-driven decisions that undermine control over our platform direction.

## 3. OKR cascade

> **Objective:** Build a reliable, scalable and business-led platform for profitable multi-brand growth.
>
> - **KR1:** Reliability: Maintain at least 99.9% availability across critical ordering, payment and store operations journeys, meeting agreed recovery targets for critical incidents.
> - **KR2:** Modernisation: Reduce critical legacy dependencies and platform complexity by x%, without compromising operational continuity.
> - **KR3:** Business value & control: . Ensure 100% of strategic platform investments have measurable business outcomes, accountable business owners and post-implementation value reviews.
