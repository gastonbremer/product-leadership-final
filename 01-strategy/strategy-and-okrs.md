## 1. Playing to Win cascade

Our ambition

Enable profitable multi-brand growth through scalable technology, differentiated customer experiences and increasingly efficient franchise operations.

Where to play
- Multiple foodservice brands and, over time, multiple countries.
- Consumers, franchisees and store employees.
- Shared commodity capabilities and differentiated brand experiences.

How to win
- Buy best-fit technology rather than building everything ourselves.
- Differentiate customer experiences while sharing common capabilities.
- Orchestrate a flexible ecosystem of strategic suppliers.
- Modernise progressively, guided by business value.
- Leverage group synergies where they create demonstrable value.

Capabilities required
- Multi-brand product management.
- Platform and enterprise architecture.
- Integration and API management.
- Vendor and ecosystem management.
- Franchise operations expertise.
- Data, AI and digital growth.

Management systems
- Value-based portfolio management.
- Run / Grow / Transform investment management.
- Architecture and supplier governance.
- Outcome-based product management.
- Business value tracking.

Strategic priorities
1. Enable multibrand commerce growth.
2. Simplify and professionalise store operations.
3. Build a scalable, modern and less supplier-dependent technology ecosystem.
